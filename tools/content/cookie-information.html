globalwms.com uses cookies only as described below. You can accept or decline them using the cookie bar, and you can change your choice at any time.

Disclaimer Pop-up Cookie

To prevent the pop-up from appearing every time you visit the site, a cookie is created that tells whether or not to display the popup.

This cookie is called gwms_notice. It is stored only if you accept cookies, and it lasts 30 days. If you decline cookies, your acceptance of the notice is remembered only until you close your browser tab, so the notice appears again on your next visit.

Cookie Choice Cookie

When you accept or decline cookies, your choice is stored in a cookie called gwms_cookies so that we do not ask you again. It lasts 12 months.

Analytics and Advertising

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies, and it does not use any third-party cookies.

Changing Your Choice

You can change your cookie choice at any time.
