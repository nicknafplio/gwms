globalwms.com uses cookies only as described below. You can accept or decline them using the cookie bar, and you can change your choice at any time.

Disclaimer Pop-up Cookie

To prevent the pop-up from appearing every time you visit the site, a cookie is created that tells whether or not to display the popup.

This cookie is called gwms_notice. It is stored only if you accept cookies, and it lasts 30 days. If you decline cookies, your acceptance of the notice is remembered only until you close your browser tab, so the notice appears again on your next visit.

Cookie Choice Cookie

When you accept or decline cookies, your choice is stored in a cookie called gwms_cookies so that we do not ask you again. It lasts 12 months.

Analytics Cookies

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics (Google Analytics 4) on each page of this website to help us understand how the site is used and to improve the visitor experience. Google Analytics sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_4F1S9L903X, which give your browser a random identifier and last up to 2 years. We do not use this information to identify you personally. We have turned off Google signals and advertising personalisation features. If you decline cookies, Google Analytics is not loaded and no Google Analytics cookies are set; if you later change your choice to decline, any Google Analytics cookies we can reach are removed.

Information collected by Google Analytics is sent to Google, which may process it outside the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area.
