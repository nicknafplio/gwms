Built on the foundations first laid in 2013 Resilience have been calmly managing discretionary portfolios for private clients, trustees and institutions throughout an increasingly volatile global economic backdrop. In 2024 the directors of the business completed a Management Buyout and reformed the company as Resilience Asset Management.

Resilience currently manage approximately $500m assets for their clients, including a significant portfolio for the Isle of Man government.

Resilience has a core belief that investment returns and sustainability are not mutually exclusive. Climate and Biodiversity are central to Resilience’s investment decision making processes. Resilience’ focus is on the future, they know that the investment decisions they make today will have consequence in the world next generations inhabit, their goal is to ensure that their impact is positive.

Resilience are committed to managing their client portfolios in alignment to the Paris Agreement and in harmony with nature. As a business they set aside 10% of our Resilient Strategies management fees for conservation.

If your clients are fortunate enough to have generated or inherited significant wealth, Resilience will help to preserve and grow their capital sustainably for the future, doing this through 7 interwoven sustainable themes that comprise their 4 resilient strategies, whilst enabling your business to meet its fiduciary and regulatory responsibilities.
