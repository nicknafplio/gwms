The Levendi Thornbridge Defined Return Fund is an actively managed portfolio of defined return investments developed by Levendi Investment Management. The Fund aims to generate positive returns of SONIA + 6.00% in all but significant and sustained market falls with less volatility and market beta than major global equity markets. The Fund also has a focus on providing a significant degree of capital protection, designed to augment wealth preservation for its investors. Since inception, the Fund has had more years of positive performance than any of the markets on which it is based.

Levendi Investment Management has a proven track record offering defensive yet diversified equity market exposure, via defined return investments. The performance of the Levendi Thornbridge Defined Return Fund places it in the top decile of Absolute Return Funds in the UK.

The Fund’s investment process combines a top-down consensus-based macro estimate of the returns of major capital markets with a robust quantitative framework which facilitates the ability to develop and value individual investments. The Fund offers:

  • Defensive yet diversified equity market exposure
  • Positive returns that don't rely on rising markets
  • Proven long term returns
  • Lower volatility than direct equity investments
  • Utilises SPIRE, a 100% proprietary and forward-looking risk analytics tool
  • Simple, consistent and low-cost strategy
  • Proven, award-winning strategy
