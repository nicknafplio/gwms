Pecora Capital is a global investment management firm that aims to provide superior risk-adjusted returns by investing across a broad and expanding range of asset classes. An owner-run company, Pecora is independent and not subject to the constraints of a large corporation or bank.

Investment Strategy

The Pecora Arena Capital Strategic Short Duration Fund aims to protect capital and deliver strong risk-adjusted returns through fundamental, bottom-up credit analysis and active portfolio management. The strategy primarily invests in U.S. and Canadian high yield bonds, convertibles, and leveraged loans, maintaining an average duration of 1–2 years and maturity of 3–5 years. Exposure is capped at 5% per issuer and 20% per industry to ensure diversification. Leverage of up to 50% may be employed to enhance returns.

Objective

To preserve investors’ capital over the long term and to generate regular income as well as a target return of SOFR + 4-6% with low volatility.

The cornerstones of the strategy – Reasons to invest

  • Active Approach: Seeks to increase yield for the same quality or increase quality for the same yield, active trading - not buy and hold strategy
  • Opportunistic Investing: Expand universe of potential investments to include securities with similar return profiles (leveraged loans, busted convertible bonds etc.)
  • Intensive Research: Fundamental analysis with an emphasis on analyzing bond structures
