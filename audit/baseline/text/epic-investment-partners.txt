Why EPIC Wealth Fund?
The EPIC Wealth Fund is a balanced strategy, blending growth with a institutional risk framework to deliver high risk adjusted returns. The portfolio invests across a range of global asset classes and sectors to effectively diversify the portfolio whilst still providing the potential for growth.
Diversified fund with a differentiated active approach
Actively managed - with flexible asset allocations according to macro views and quantitative analysis expressed through best of breed active managers;
Designed to maximise risk-adjusted returns at competitive cost.
Broad investment universe
Provides access to a wide range of asset classes, investment styles, sectors and regions;
Asset classes include equities, fixed income (across sovereign and credit markets), commodities, alternatives and property.
Investment Team
Pushpanshu Prakash - Fund Manager
Pushpanshu is the Fund Manager of EPIC’s UK domiciled range of multi asset funds, the EPIC Wealth Fund as well as EPIC’s range of model portfolio solutions. Pushpanshu joined EPIC in March 2019 from City Financial where he was a fund analyst for the Multi Asset team responsible for fund specific research and quantitative modelling. He joined City Financial in 2017 after graduating from University College London (UCL) with an MSci in Mathematics, where he completed his thesis in fluid dynamics with a focus on situational modelling and financial mathematics.
Simon Newell – Fund Manager
Simon manages EPIC’s fund of hedge funds, Master Capital Fund Limited. He has been analysing and investing in funds for over 25 years, working with institutions, family offices and HNWI. He joined Master Capital in 2007 and has been Fund Manager since 2012. He previously worked at Fox-Pitt, Kelton, from 2004 to 2007, as an analyst covering the European Financials sector. He was a funds analyst at Flemings / J.P. Morgan from 1997 to 2003. Prior to that, he worked for a commercial property company, Insignia ESG, in New York, from 1996 to 1997, on research systems design and development. He is a Mathematics graduate from Loughborough University.
