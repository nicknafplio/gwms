Exceptional wealth management solutions
Established in 2003, Global Wealth Management Solutions (GWMS) provides access to a suite of best of breed financial products. We are trusted by banks, institutions, captives, trust companies, family offices and boutique fund managers, who value our exceptional market knowledge, excellent service levels and strong track record.
Our management team has over 150 years of financial services experience and provides the highest levels of support, professionalism and integrity. Most of our Partners and senior staff have been with us for more than 10 years. As a result, we have developed a sustainable and successful business, based on longevity, experience, security and value.
Locations
We offer a global reach combined with local knowledge, transacting business in over 80 countries. GWMS is headquartered in the UK but reaches a global audience through our teams operating in Offshore Islands and throughout Europe, Africa, Asia, The Middle East, Caribbean Islands and South America. We understand each marketplace, its nuances, challenges and opportunities, utilising the deep insights of our country specialists.
