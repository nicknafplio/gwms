Built on a foundation of strength, flexibility, and integrity, the company entered a new chapter in 2025 with a strategic management buyout—further enhancing its independence, focus, and long-term stability.

Why Guernsey?
The jurisdiction of Guernsey was chosen with intent and foresight. Known for its world-class regulatory environment, Guernsey offers one of the highest levels of investor protection globally.
Unlike other jurisdictions, the Guernsey Financial Services Commission (GFSC) mandates that at least 90% of client assets be held in trust by an independent third-party trustee. At Veritas, we go even further.
100% Client Asset Protection
Veritas commits to placing 100% of all client assets into a master trust—ensuring these assets are entirely segregated from the insurance company’s balance sheet and fully ring-fenced for maximum investor protection.
Client assets are securely held under the Sovereign Trustees Master Trust, part of a globally respected fiduciary business managing over $20 billion in assets under administration.
